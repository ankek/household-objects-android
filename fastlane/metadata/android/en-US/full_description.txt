HHO (Household Objects) is a client for the self-hosted HHO home inventory server.

Keep track of what your household owns: what is in the garage, which box holds the holiday lights, whether the drill is still under warranty.

* Works offline and syncs with your own HHO server when a connection is available
* Scan barcodes and QR codes with the camera to find and update items quickly
* No cloud account and no subscription: your data stays on your hardware

This app needs an HHO server that you run yourself.
